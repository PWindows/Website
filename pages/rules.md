## 1. Be Respectful
Treat all players with respect. No harassment, bullying, or discriminatio, death threats, sexism, hate speech, racism (**no N-word**, this includes the soft N-word). Do not doxx, swatt, and witch hunt anyone, spend your time doing something useful instead. If you have a problem with someone, please report it to staff in the [support portal](https://support.pwindows.qzz.io/).

## 2. No Cheating
Using hacks, exploits, to gain an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited. This includes any kind of X-ray resource pack/client, freecam, player outlines, or smart automations where the player isn't actually playing such as Baritone and its forks. Macros of complex kinds are also not allowed (any macro that uses input from the game such as the display), but simple ones like autoclickers are allowed. Anything that gives you or another player/person(including multiple) a personal/competitive/overall advantage is not allowed.

## 3. No Spam & Advertising
Excessive messaging, advertising or spam in chat is not allowed. For example, you should not send multiple messages with the same intended meaning repeatedly.

## 4. Listen to Staff
Staff members have the final say. Please respect their decisions. If they say you have broken a rule, you probably have. If you think you have more to appeal, contact us, don't post it online and try to cancel us just because it was a 'small offense'. 

## 5. Have fun!
You are in this server because you want to have fun and spend your time on meaningful things. Don't break the rules, and you'll have lots of fun playing here. Good luck, and have fun!
